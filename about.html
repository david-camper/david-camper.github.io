BIO

Excalibur

With a passion for authenticity and a willingness to stand out, rapper, producer and Poet Laureate: Excalibur, breathes life through his art. Born in Vallejo, California, but a native of Fairfield, Excalibur began his artistic journey at fifteen writing rap verses and short stories. He has since released three full length albums, numerous EPs and creates worlds through his music. Starting as early as his 2020 release, 'STARCHILD', his music tackles love, life, purpose and the challenge of becoming his own hero in a loveless world. He now performs across northern California as the first, ever Poet Laureate of Suisun City.

Excalibur's musical influences range from hip-hop icons like KYLE, Kendrick Lamar, Lil Wayne and A$AP Rocky to genre bending sounds of the ilk of Very Rare, Frank Ocean, Panic! At the Disco, Beabadoobie and others in the intersection of indie, pop punk and alternative RnB. In 2017 he uploaded his first single to SoundCloud, followed by a series of tracks that same year, with his first EP: “King of Hearts” coming in mid-2018. Throughout 2019 he released multiple EPs, including ‘50 Reasons to Smile’, ‘707’ and ‘Summer Slaps Vol. 1’, displaying his ambitious range of genres: from soft spoken indie and experimental sounds, to grandiose west coast bangers complete with intricately crafted rap verses. In 2019 he became a member of MADDRAMA Performance Troupe and Outspoken Arts Collective during his collegiate journey at Jackson State University, connecting with artists across the nation in a hotspot of culture and musical talent.

In 2021, the heartbroken hero sage tackled his most ambitious project at that point with ‘Ikigai’; a journey of purpose, love and self-worth; expanding on the Japanese philosophy of that which brings life’s value. Just before the release, Excalibur was named 2020's top male HBCU lyricist by Thurgood Marshall College Fund's RISE homecoming cypher competition, gaining him a nod from legendary Mississippi rapper, producer and actor David Banner. He would later earn a spot as a finalist in TMCF's 2021 RISE Homecoming Cypher, performing alongside multi-platinum, super-producer Jazze Pha in Atlanta, GA as part of the organization's annual homecoming livestream; displaying his lyrical poise alongside a group of HBCU-bred rappers and poets.

As his hunger in rap and desire to pluck heartstrings in the alternative spaces of music expanded, the Starchild kept poetry close to his heart. After graduating with his bachelors and returning to his hometown of Suisun City, CA he continued to perform in Solano County and built strong bonds with local artists, businesses and city officials. In 2022, he would submit his poetry to the city of Suisun and be formally appointed as Poet Laureate of Suisun City, receiving a congressional commendation by congressmen John Garamendi in the process. In that same year, Excalibur's discography grew with a slew of new singles as well as two new EPs: ‘THINGS FALL APART’ and ‘X's & O's’. By 2023, he had reached the fourth installment of his ‘Summer Slaps’ series, giving us his iconic single ‘Life's Too $hort’. He would follow this up with the release of his next full-length album: ‘ROMANCE DAWN’, a storybook of romantic relationships and life-lessons told across numerous perspectives. This project would serve as a sharpening of the many different styles that he had honed across his journey, showcased by the project's lead single: ‘HIT THE LIGHTS!’, a pop punk examination of young adulthood for American youth over sullen guitar leads and banging drums.

As Poet Laureate of Suisun City, Excalibur has produced community arts and music events, both independently and in collaboration with nonprofits, public officials, and local businesses. He has led the way in organizing artist showcases in Suisun City including ‘OFF THE RECORD I, II and III’, ‘Got Soul?’ and ‘Fire & Water’ building a platform for other local Bay Area musicians and poets. His collaborative work as an organizer helped bring to life Suisun City’s 2024 Community Climate Resilience Fair in partnership with Sustainable Solano and 2025's Resilient Roots: Climate Leaders Summit in partnership with GreenBelt Alliance. During this time, the Starchild has performed alongside notable names in Bay Area music and arts, including Ravyn Justice, DJ Rick Lee, The Federation, California State Poet Laureate Lee Herrick and Grammy-nominated spoken word poet Shawn William. His work culminated with his ‘SATISFACTION EP’ in 2026, giving the world: ‘Princess Tiana’, a heartfelt ballad built from swelling strings, longing acoustic guitar and poetically sharp rap verses. Now a masters graduate of UC Berkeley, he performs across the Bay Area, carrying forth his conviction as the first ever Poet Laureate of Suisun City and rap’s next heartbroken hero of legend.
